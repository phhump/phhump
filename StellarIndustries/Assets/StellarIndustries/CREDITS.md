## Planet and sky textures
Solar System Scope — https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/ — licensed under
**Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**. Based on NASA imagery.
Files in `Resources/Textures/` (resized): earth_day, earth_night, earth_clouds, moon, mars, sun, jupiter, milky_way,
exo_rock_a (Ceres, fictional), exo_ice (Eris, fictional), exo_rock_b (Haumea, fictional), exo_rock_c (Makemake, fictional), exo_hot (Venus surface).
Exoplanet surfaces are **artist's impressions**: no images of these worlds exist; textures of other bodies are tinted by the host star's colour.
